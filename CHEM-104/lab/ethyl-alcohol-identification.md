# Ethyl Alcohol (EtOH) — Identification

## Aim

To identify ethyl alcohol (EtOH) by characteristic chemical tests.

## Principle / Theory

Ethyl alcohol is identified through ester formation with acetic acid (fruity smell of ethyl acetate), a **positive** iodoform test (yellow precipitate with a characteristic smell), oxidation to acetaldehyde, and ester formation with salicylic acid.

## Required Reagents

- Ethyl alcohol (C₂H₅OH)
- Anhydrous CH₃COOH
- Concentrated H₂SO₄
- Iodine (I₂), NaOH/KOH
- Potassium dichromate (K₂Cr₂O₇), concentrated H₂SO₄
- Salicylic acid

## Apparatus

- Test tubes

## Procedure

1. **Ethyl Acetate Test:** EtOH + anhydrous CH₃COOH + concentrated H₂SO₄, heat (Δ).
2. **Iodoform Test:** EtOH + I₂ + NaOH/KOH, heat (Δ).
3. **Acetaldehyde Test:** EtOH + K₂Cr₂O₇ + concentrated H₂SO₄, heat (Δ).
4. **Ethyl Salicylate Test:** EtOH + salicylic acid + concentrated H₂SO₄, heat (Δ).

## Chemical Reactions

**1. Ethyl acetate test**

$$
C_2H_5OH + CH_3COOH \xrightarrow{conc.\ H_2SO_4,\ \Delta} CH_3COOC_2H_5 + H_2O
$$

Fruity smell of ethyl acetate.

Related step noted in source:

$$
2CH_3COONa + H_2SO_4 \rightarrow 2CH_3COOH + Na_2SO_4
$$

**2. Iodoform test**

$$
C_2H_5OH + 4I_2 + 6NaOH \rightarrow CHI_3\downarrow + HCOONa + 5NaI + 5H_2O
$$

Yellow precipitate of iodoform; smell of iodoform present — positive test.

**3. Acetaldehyde test**

$$
CH_3CH_2OH + [O] \rightarrow CH_3CHO + H_2O
$$

Green colour solution (from K₂Cr₂O₇/H₂SO₄ oxidant) and smell of acetaldehyde.

**4. Ethyl salicylate test**

$$
CH_3CH_2OH + \text{(salicylic acid)} \xrightarrow{conc.\ H_2SO_4,\ \Delta} \text{ethyl salicylate} + H_2O
$$

Source records "no smell" for this test [unclear in source: whether this indicates a negative or simply weak result].

## Observations

| Test | Reagent/Condition | Observation | Conclusion |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ethyl acetate test | Anhydrous CH₃COOH + conc. H₂SO₄, heat | Fruity smell of ethyl acetate | Positive — confirms –OH |
| Iodoform test | I₂ + NaOH/KOH, heat | Yellow ppt., smell of iodoform present | **Positive** — distinguishes EtOH from MeOH |
| Acetaldehyde test | K₂Cr₂O₇ + conc. H₂SO₄, heat | Green colour solution, smell of acetaldehyde | Ethanol oxidised to acetaldehyde |
| Ethyl salicylate test | Salicylic acid + conc. H₂SO₄, heat | No smell (per source) | [unclear in source] |

## Notes / Observations from Source

- The iodoform test is the key positive distinguishing test for ethanol (negative for methanol).
