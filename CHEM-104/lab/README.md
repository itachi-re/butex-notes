## Source Fidelity Notes

- All chemical equations, reagent names, numerical values, and experimental sequences are reproduced from the handwritten source.
- Genuinely illegible or ambiguous handwriting is marked `[unclear in source]` rather than guessed.
- Where the source's own arithmetic appears internally inconsistent (e.g. two slightly different intermediate figures), both values are preserved with a **Source note** rather than silently reconciled.
