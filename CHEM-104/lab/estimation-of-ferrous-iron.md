## Determination of % Error

$$
\%\,\text{error}
=
\frac{\text{Actual conc. of }KMnO_4-\text{Experimental conc. of }KMnO_4}
{\text{Actual conc. of }KMnO_4}
\times 100
$$

$$
=
\frac{0.02-0.0199}{0.02}\times100
=
0.5\%
$$

> **N.B.:** After the experiment, wash the burette with FeSO₄ solution.

## Experiment 3: Estimation of Fe²⁺ with Standard KMnO₄ Solution

### Essential Chemicals

- $KMnO_4$
- Mohr's salt — $FeSO_4\cdot(NH_4)_2SO_4\cdot6H_2O$
- Green vitriol — $FeSO_4\cdot7H_2O$
- 1 M $H_2SO_4$

### Essential Equipment

Burette, pipette, volumetric flask, conical flask, funnel, pipette filler, etc.

### Molecular Weights

| Compound | Molecular Weight (g/mol) |
|---|---:|
| $KMnO_4$ | 158 |
| $FeSO_4\cdot(NH_4)_2SO_4\cdot6H_2O$ (Mohr's salt) | 392 |
| $FeSO_4\cdot7H_2O$ | 278 |

---

## Preparation of Solutions

### Step 1: Preparation of 500 mL 0.02 M (M/50) KMnO₄ Solution

1000 mL of 0.02 M $KMnO_4$ solution contains:

$$
158\times0.02=3.16\text{ g }KMnO_4
$$

Therefore, 500 mL of 0.02 M $KMnO_4$ solution contains:

$$
\frac{158\times500\times0.02}{1000}
=
1.58\text{ g }KMnO_4
$$

**Actual concentration of KMnO₄:**

$$
\frac{0.0206}{0.0200}\times0.02
\approx0.0206\text{ M}
$$

> **Note:** The exact handwritten value is unclear.

### Step 2: Preparation of 500 mL 1 M H₂SO₄ Solution

Using concentrated $H_2SO_4$ of approximately 18 M:

$$
V_1M_1=V_2M_2
$$

$$
V_1\times18=500\times1
$$

$$
V_1=\frac{500}{18}=27.77\text{ mL}
$$

Therefore, **27.77 mL of concentrated $H_2SO_4$** is required and the solution is made up to **500 mL**.

### Step 3: Preparation of 250 mL 0.1 M Mohr's Salt Solution

For $FeSO_4\cdot(NH_4)_2SO_4\cdot6H_2O$:

$$
\text{Required mass}
=
\frac{392\times250\times0.1}{1000}
=
9.8\text{ g}
$$

**Handwritten procedure:** Take **10 g Mohr's salt** in a **250 mL volumetric flask**, dissolve it in **200 mL of 1 M $H_2SO_4$**, and then make up the volume with **50 mL $H_2O$**.

## Step 4: Titration of Ferrous Salt Solution with Standard KMnO₄

Take **20 mL ferrous salt solution** and add approximately **5 mL conc. $H_2SO_4$**. Titrate against standard $KMnO_4$ solution from the burette.

**End point:** Light violet/pink colour persists.

> **N.B.:** Conc. $H_2SO_4$ is added to prevent oxidation of $Fe^{2+}$ by air.

### Burette Readings

| Trial | Initial Reading (mL) | Final Reading (mL) | Difference (mL) |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 0.0 | 4.0 | 4.0 |
| 2 | 4.0 | 8.1 | 4.1 |
| 3 | 8.1 | 12.0 | 3.9 |

Average titre value:

$$
\frac{4.0+4.1+3.9}{3}
=
4.0\text{ mL}
$$

# Chemistry–II: Calculations

## Reactions

### Decomposition of KMnO₄ in Acidic Medium

$$
2KMnO_4+3H_2SO_4
\rightarrow
K_2SO_4+2MnSO_4+3H_2O+5[O]
$$

### Oxidation of Ferrous Sulphate

$$
10FeSO_4+5H_2SO_4+5[O]
\rightarrow
5Fe_2(SO_4)_3+5H_2O
$$

### Combined Molecular Equation

$$
2KMnO_4+8H_2SO_4+10FeSO_4
\rightarrow
K_2SO_4+2MnSO_4+5Fe_2(SO_4)_3+8H_2O
$$

## Ionic Equation

$$
2MnO_4^-+16H^++10Fe^{2+}
\rightarrow
2Mn^{2+}+10Fe^{3+}+8H_2O
$$

### Half-Reactions

**Reduction:**

$$
2MnO_4^-+16H^++10e^-
\rightarrow
2Mn^{2+}+8H_2O
$$

**Oxidation:**

$$
10Fe^{2+}
\rightarrow
10Fe^{3+}+10e^-
$$
