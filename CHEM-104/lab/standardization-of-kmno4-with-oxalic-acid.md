# Standardization of KMnO₄ Solution with Standard Oxalic Acid

## Aim

Standardization of KMnO₄ solution using standard oxalic acid solution (a secondary standard).

## Required Reagents

- KMnO₄
- Oxalic acid (COOH·COOH·2H₂O)
- H₂SO₄

## Apparatus

- Burette
- Pipette
- Volumetric flask
- Funnel
- Conical flask
- Pipette filler

## Procedure

### Step 1: Preparation of 250 ml 0.02M KMnO₄ (secondary standard)

- Molecular weight of KMnO₄ = 158.08
- 1000 ml of 1M KMnO₄ solution contains 158.08 g KMnO₄
- ∴ 250 ml of 0.02M solution requires: 158.08 × 250 × 0.02 / 1000 = **0.79 g**
- The KMnO₄ solution is boiled for 15–30 minutes, then cooled, filtered through glass wool, and stored in a coloured bottle in a dark place.

### Step 2: Preparation of 250 ml, 0.05M oxalic acid solution

- Molecular weight of oxalic acid (COOH·COOH·2H₂O) = 126
- 250 ml of 0.05M oxalic acid solution requires: 126 × 250 × 0.05 / 1000 = 1.575 g
- Actual strength of oxalic acid = (weight taken × concentration) / weight to be taken = (1.586 × 0.05M) / 1.575 = **0.05035 M**

> **Source note:** The weight-taken value (1.586 g) differs slightly from the calculated required weight (1.575 g); this is reproduced from the handwritten source without correction.

### Step 3: Preparation of 250 ml 2M H₂SO₄ solution

Using S₁V₁(conc. H₂SO₄) = S₂V₂(dilute H₂SO₄):

$$
V_1 = \frac{250\ \text{ml} \times 2M}{18.40M} = 27.17\ \text{ml}
$$

[unclear in source: a side annotation near this step, referencing "78% H₂SO₄" and a separate HCl dilution note, is not clearly legible and is omitted.]

### Step 4: Titration of KMnO₄ solution with standard oxalic acid solution (oxidation–reduction reaction)

Procedure: 10 ml oxalic acid + 20 ml 2M H₂SO₄ + 3 drops KMnO₄ solution, heated and stirred until the colour disappears (60–70°C); KMnO₄ is then added again until a light violet colour persists — this is the end point.

| Trial | Volume of oxalic acid (ml) | Burette 1st reading | Burette final reading | Difference | Average reading | Concentration of KMnO₄ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 20 ml (10 ml oxalic acid + 2M H₂SO₄, 3 drops KMnO₄, heat) | | | | 10.1 | |

> Only a single average titre value (10.1 ml) is present in the source; individual first/final burette readings for repeat trials are not tabulated in the handwritten notes.

### Step 5: Calculation

Reaction:

$$
2KMnO_4 + 3H_2SO_4 + 5H_2C_2O_4 \rightarrow K_2SO_4 + 2MnSO_4 + 8H_2O + 10CO_2
$$

$$
\therefore 2KMnO_4 \equiv 5H_2C_2O_4
$$

By molar equivalence:

$$
\frac{S_1V_1(\text{oxalic acid})}{S_2V_2(\text{KMnO}_4)} = \frac{5}{2}
$$

$$
\Rightarrow S_2(\text{KMnO}_4) = \frac{2 \times S_1V_1(\text{oxalic acid})}{5 \times V_2(\text{KMnO}_4)} = \frac{2 \times 0.05035 \times 10}{5 \times 10.1} = 0.0199\ M
$$

### Step 6: Determination of Error

$$
\%\ \text{error} = \frac{\text{Actual conc. of KMnO}_4 - \text{Experimental conc. of KMnO}_4}{\text{Actual conc. of KMnO}_4} \times 100
$$

$$
= \frac{0.02 - 0.0199}{0.02} \times 100\% = 0.5\%
$$

## Result

The standardized concentration of KMnO₄ was found to be **0.0199 M**, against an intended concentration of 0.02 M, giving a percentage error of **0.5%**.

## Precautions

- After the experiment, wash the burette with FeSO₄ solution.
