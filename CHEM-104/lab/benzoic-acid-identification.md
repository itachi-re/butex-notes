# Benzoic Acid — Identification

## Aim

To identify benzoic acid (C₆H₅COOH) by its physical properties and characteristic chemical tests.

## Principle / Theory

Benzoic acid is identified via silver benzoate precipitation with AgNO₃, its reversible precipitation/dissolution behaviour with Denigè's solution, a ferric chloride colour test, and ester formation with ethanol.

## Required Reagents

- Benzoic acid (C₆H₅COOH)
- Sodium hydroxide (NaOH)
- Silver nitrate (AgNO₃)
- Denigè's solution
- Ferric chloride (FeCl₃) solution
- Hydrochloric acid (HCl)
- Ethyl alcohol (C₂H₅OH), concentrated H₂SO₄
- Nitric acid (HNO₃) [unclear in source: exact role/product]

## Apparatus

- Test tubes

## Physical Properties (from source)

Benzoic acid is a white solid, mp 122°C, crystallising from water in pearly flakes. It is sparingly soluble in cold water but dissolves readily in hot water, ether, ethanol, and benzene. It is steam-volatile and may be sublimed.

## Procedure

1. **Action with AgNO₃:** Neutralise benzoic acid with NaOH, then react with AgNO₃.
2. **Action with Denigè's solution:** React neutral benzoic acid solution with Denigè's solution; heat, then cool.
3. **Ferric chloride test:** React neutral benzoic acid solution with FeCl₃ solution, then add HCl.
4. **Action with C₂H₅OH:** Benzoic acid + ethanol + concentrated H₂SO₄, heat.

## Chemical Reactions

**1. Action with AgNO₃**

$$
C_6H_5COOH + NaOH \xrightarrow{\text{neutralised}} C_6H_5COONa + H_2O
$$

$$
C_6H_5COONa + AgNO_3 \rightarrow C_6H_5COOAg\downarrow + NaNO_3
$$

White precipitate of silver benzoate; the precipitate dissolves on heating and reappears on cooling.

**2. Ferric chloride test**

$$
3C_6H_5COONa + 2FeCl_3 + 3H_2O \rightarrow [Fe(C_6H_5COO)_3 \cdot Fe(OH)_3]\downarrow + 3NaCl + 3HCl
$$

A buff/khaki-coloured precipitate forms; on adding HCl, a pale yellow precipitate forms.

$$
[Fe(C_6H_5COO)_3 \cdot Fe(OH)_3] + 6HCl \rightarrow 2FeCl_3 + 3C_6H_5COOH + 3H_2O
$$

**3. Action with ethanol (ester formation)**

$$
C_6H_5COOH + C_2H_5OH \xrightarrow{conc.\ H_2SO_4,\ \Delta} C_6H_5COOC_2H_5 \text{ (ethyl benzoate)} + H_2O
$$

## Observations

| Test | Reagent/Condition | Observation | Conclusion |
|---|---|---|---|
| Silver nitrate test | NaOH (neutralise) + AgNO₃ | White ppt.; dissolves on heating, reappears on cooling | Positive — silver benzoate formed |
| Denigè's solution test | Neutral soln + Denigè's soln, heat/cool | White ppt. disappears on heating, reappears on cooling | Confirmatory |
| Ferric chloride test | FeCl₃ soln, then HCl | Buff/khaki ppt., then pale yellow ppt. with HCl | Positive — confirms carboxylate |
| Ester formation | C₂H₅OH + conc. H₂SO₄, Δ | Ethyl benzoate formed | Positive for –COOH |
