# Methyl Alcohol vs Ethyl Alcohol — Comparative Properties & Identification

## Aim

To compare the physical properties and identification-test results of methyl alcohol (MeOH) and ethyl alcohol (EtOH).

*Reference cited in source: Advanced Organic Chemistry, B.S. Bahl and Arun Bahl, 2nd edition.*

## Physical Properties

| Property | Methyl Alcohol | Ethyl Alcohol |
|---|---|---|
| Appearance | Colourless, sweet-smelling liquid | Colourless, volatile, sweet-smelling liquid |
| Boiling point | 64.5°C | 78.3°C |
| Specific gravity | 0.79 (at 0°C) | 0.789 |
| Solubility in water | Soluble in all proportions | Soluble in all proportions |
| Other solubility | Suitable solvent for oils and fats | Soluble in C₆H₆, ether, CHCl₃; best solvent for all organic compounds |

## Uses of Methyl Alcohol (from source)

1. Industrial production of HCHO (formaldehyde)
2. Laboratory solvent
3. Antifreezing agent for engines
4. To produce methylated spirit
5. [unclear in source — fifth listed use left blank/incomplete in the handwritten notes]

## Comparative Identification Test Table

| Reagent / Test | Methyl Alcohol — Observation | Methyl Alcohol — Reaction | Ethyl Alcohol — Observation | Ethyl Alcohol — Reaction |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Iodoform test: alcohol + I₂ + NaOH, Δ | No reaction | — | Yellow ppt. of iodoform | $C_2H_5OH + 4I_2 + 6NaOH \rightarrow CHI_3\downarrow + HCOONa + 5NaI + 5H_2O$ |
| Formation of aldehyde: alcohol + K₂Cr₂O₇ + H₂SO₄, Δ | Smell of HCHO present | $CH_3OH + [O] \rightarrow HCHO + H_2O$ | Green colour solution; smell of acetaldehyde present | $CH_3CH_2OH + [O] \rightarrow CH_3CHO + H_2O$ |
| Formation of acetate: alcohol + anhydrous CH₃COOH + conc. H₂SO₄, Δ | No smell; produces methyl acetate | $CH_3OH + CH_3COOH \rightarrow CH_3COOCH_3 + H_2O$ | Fruity smell of ethyl acetate present | $C_2H_5OH + CH_3COOH \rightarrow CH_3COOC_2H_5 + H_2O$ |
| Salicylic acid + conc. H₂SO₄, Δ | Smell of methyl salicylate present | $CH_3OH + \text{salicylic acid} \rightarrow \text{methyl salicylate} + H_2O$ | No smell (per source) | $C_2H_5OH + \text{salicylic acid} \rightarrow \text{ethyl salicylate} + H_2O$ |

## Notes / Observations from Source

- Source page references: p.380–381 of the cited reference.
- The iodoform test is the single most decisive distinguishing test between the two alcohols: negative for methanol, positive for ethanol.
